Management Notes

    Methodology

    YoY calculation: 2026 actual month compared with the same month in 2025.
    YTD calculation: Sum of available 2026 months and same-period 2025 months.
    NEV definition: EV, PHV, FCV and selected plug-in combinations are classified as NEV. HV and MHV are classified as Fuel-powered / Traditional Hybrids (Non-NEV).
    Ranking: Top brands and OEMs are sorted by 2026 YTD actual volume under the selected filters.
